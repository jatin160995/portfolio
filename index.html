GO Bus
First-of-its-kind online bus booking platform in Cameroon with a unique payment system.
Mobile AppBackendAfrica
🔄 In Development
The Challenge

Cameroon had no reliable digital solution for intercity bus booking. Travellers relied entirely on physical ticket counters, with no way to book in advance, check availability, or pay online — especially challenging given the unique payment infrastructure in the region.

What I Built

A complete, full-stack bus booking system from scratch — Flutter mobile app for passengers, Laravel-powered backend, MySQL database, and a custom-built API layer. The payment system was designed specifically for the Cameroonian market, making it the first of its kind in the country.
